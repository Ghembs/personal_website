# Recordings

The files in this folder come from [audio-cmn](https://github.com/hugolpz/audio-cmn)
(Hugo Lopez, PLIDAM, INALCO; recording software by Nicolas Vion), in its 24 kbps
version, copied and renamed by `scripts/build-audio.mjs`.

| Folder | Content | Speaker | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `syl/` | every syllable of Mandarin in its four tones (`ma3.mp3`, ü written `v`) | Chen Wang | [CC BY-SA](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/) |
| `w/` | the HSK 2000 words and characters used by this section (`爸爸.mp3`) | Yue Tan ([Shtooka](http://packs.shtooka.net/cmn-caen-tan/readme.txt)) | [CC BY-SA](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/) |

These recordings are shared under the same licence. `index.json` lists them,
with the pinyin of the texts of the introduction that have no recording.
