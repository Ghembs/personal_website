# Data licences

The files in this folder are generated by `scripts/build-hanzi.mjs`. Each comes
from a different source and keeps that source's licence, which is why they are
kept apart.

| File | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| `hsk.json` | Characters (认读字) and words of HSK levels 1–4 from the HSK syllabus (HSK 考试大纲), 中外语言交流合作中心, 2025 | lists of facts taken from the syllabus; the syllabus itself is not redistributed |
| `cedict.json` | [CC-CEDICT](https://cc-cedict.org/), MDBG | [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/): this file is shared under the same licence |
| `decomp.json` | `dictionary.txt` of [Make Me a Hanzi](https://github.com/skishore/makemeahanzi), derived from Unihan and CJKlib | [LGPL-3.0](licenses/LGPL-3.0.txt) (with the [GPL-3.0](licenses/GPL-3.0.txt) it builds on) |

Stroke-order data is not hosted here: [Hanzi Writer](https://hanziwriter.org/)
(MIT) loads `hanzi-writer-data` (Arphic Public License) from jsDelivr at runtime.

The font `../fonts/hanzi.woff2` is a subset of Noto Serif SC, under the
[SIL Open Font License](../fonts/OFL.txt).
